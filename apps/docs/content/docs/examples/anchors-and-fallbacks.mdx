A receiver can show less than the sender. A filter can remove a row, and a small screen can drop an action column. If you anchor one element per level, the cursor falls back to the nearest ancestor that exists. In this demo, the wide pane shows 8 orders with refund buttons. The narrow pane shows 4 orders and no buttons.

## Behavior notes

Try it: Turn off "Show orders" in the narrow pane.

- A refund button sends four anchors, most specific first: `orders.row:1042.refund`, `orders.row:1042`, `orders`, and `shop`.
- Order 1042 exists in the narrow pane, but its button does not. The cursor falls back one level to the row. The status bar shows "fallback +1", and a dashed outline marks the row.
- Order 1046 does not exist in the narrow pane. The cursor falls back two levels to the table.
- Without the orders table, the cursor falls back to the page anchor `shop`.
