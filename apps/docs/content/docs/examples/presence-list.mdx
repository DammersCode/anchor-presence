A presence list shows who is in the room, independent of where their cursor is. `useRemoteUsers` gives you that list, and it does not rerender when a cursor moves. In this demo, three users work on a roadmap board. The wide pane shows their names in the bar. The narrow pane shows only their avatars.

The demo uses short timeouts, so that you see the idle and leave steps in seconds. Keep `heartbeatMs` below `staleTimeoutMs`. If the heartbeat is slower, the store removes a connected user who does not move. The defaults are 10 s for `heartbeatMs`, 15 s for `idleAfterMs`, and 70 s for `staleTimeoutMs`.

`user` is untrusted network data. Render `user.name` as text, as the scene does, and never as HTML.

## Behavior notes

- Mira, Theo, and Jun join a few seconds apart. Each user appears in the bar when the first message from that user arrives.
- Theo stops after two moves. After `idleAfterMs` with no change, the bar dims Theo and shows "away". `CursorLayer` dims the cursor too. Heartbeats do not end the idle stage.
- Jun leaves the page. Jun's client sends a leave message, so Jun's cursor and bar entry disappear at once.
- A client that stops without a leave message stays in the bar until `staleTimeoutMs` passes with no message.
- Move your pointer in one pane. You appear in the bar of the other pane as "You".
