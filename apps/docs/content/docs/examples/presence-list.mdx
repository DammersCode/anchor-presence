A presence list shows who is in the room, independent of where their cursor is. `useRemoteUsers` gives you that list, and it does not rerender when a cursor moves. In this demo, three users work on a roadmap board. The wide pane shows their names in the bar. The narrow pane shows only their avatars.

The demo uses short timeouts, so that you see the idle and leave steps in seconds. See [Remote presence](/docs/core/remote-presence) for the defaults.

<Callout type="warn">
  If `staleTimeoutMs` is at most twice `heartbeatIntervalMs`, the store can remove a connected user who does
  not move. In development mode, `createPresenceClient` logs a warning for this setting.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  If you render `user` as HTML, another peer can inject markup into your page. `user` is untrusted network
  data. Render `user.name` as text, as the scene does.
</Callout>

## Behavior notes

Try it: Move your pointer in one pane.

- Mira, Theo, and Jun join a few seconds apart. Each user appears in the bar when the first message from that user arrives.
- Theo stops after two moves. After `idleAfterMs` with no change, the bar dims Theo and shows "idle". `CursorLayer` dims the cursor too. Heartbeats do not end the idle state.
- Jun leaves the page. Jun's client sends a leave message, so Jun's cursor and bar entry disappear at once.
- A client that stops without a leave message stays in the bar until `staleTimeoutMs` passes with no message.
- You appear in the bar of the other pane as "You".
- Screen readers skip cursors. The [Join and leave announcer](/docs/get-started/guides/join-leave-announcer) recipe adds a live region.
