An app with live cursors usually also shows who is here, independent of where their cursor is. `useRemoteUsers` gives you that list, and it does not rerender when a cursor moves. In this demo, three users work on a roadmap board. The wide pane shows their names in the bar. The narrow pane shows only their avatars.

<ExamplePreview scene="presence" />

## What's in the demo

- Mira, Theo, and Jun join a few seconds apart. Each user appears in the bar when the first message arrives.
- Theo stops after two moves. After `idleAfterMs` with no message, the bar dims Theo and shows "away". `CursorLayer` dims the cursor too.
- Jun leaves the page. The cursor disappears at once. Jun stays in the bar as idle until `staleTimeoutMs` removes the entry.
- Move your pointer in one pane. You appear in the bar of the other pane as "You".

The demo uses short timeouts, so that you see the idle and leave steps in seconds. Keep `heartbeatMs` below `idleAfterMs`. If the heartbeat is slower, a connected user who does not move goes idle between two heartbeats. The defaults are 10 s, 15 s, and 70 s.

`user` is untrusted network data. Render `user.name` as text, as the scene does, and never as HTML.
