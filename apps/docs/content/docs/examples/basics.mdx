A live cursor needs three things: a client, a provider, and at least one anchor. In this demo, the same three buttons sit in a row in the wide pane and in a column in the narrow pane. A cursor on a button in one pane lands on the same button in the other pane.

## Behavior notes

Try it: Move your pointer over a button or a line of text in one pane.

- `data-anchor` on each button: `post.save`, `post.preview`, and `post.publish`.
- The draft tag, the title, and the summary are anchors too: `post.tag`, `post.title`, `post.summary`. A point on the text resolves to that text.
- `data-anchor="post"` on the card. A point in a gap resolves to the card, not to the viewport.
- The status bar under each pane shows the tier and the anchor of the remote cursor.
- The other pane shows your cursor on the same element, at a different position.
- The buttons change size when the pane shrinks. The demo shows this worst case on purpose. For the layout rule, see [Keep item sizes and shapes alike](/docs/get-started/guides/layout-design#keep-item-sizes-and-shapes-alike).
