An anchor inside a scroll container can exist, with a real size and position, while the container clips it out of view. Without a policy for this case, the cursor floats outside the container. In this demo, the wide pane shows every thread of an inbox. The narrow pane shows the same threads in a short scroll list.

`clippedPointPolicy` hides a cursor when its point is outside the visible part of the anchor. The hint button reads the same cursor from `useRemoteCursors`. A hidden cursor keeps its `anchorId` and its `pointVisible` value, so the hint knows the thread.

## Behavior notes

Try it: Scroll the narrow list with your pointer or your keyboard, or select the button at the edge of the list.

- When the remote cursor is on thread 8, the narrow pane cannot show that thread without a scroll. The visibility policy hides the cursor, and the status bar shows "out of view".
- A button at the top or bottom edge of the list shows where the cursor is. The button scrolls the thread into view, and the cursor then appears on the thread.
- The cursor appears when its thread comes into view.
